Flash Safety Protocol & Invariant Protections

To prevent irrecoverable baseband bricking or IMEI corruption, safety locks are enforced at the transport layer.

Strict Read-Only Partitions (Locked)

These partitions store factory radio calibration data and cryptographically anchored device identifiers. Overwrite attempts are intercepted and rejected prior to USB transmission.

  • EFS / SEC_EFS: Radio calibrations, IMEI and cellular certificates.
  • CPEFS / NVDATA: Cellular baseband non-volatile parameter store.
  • TNV / PERSDATA: Factory hardware keystore trust anchors.
Permitted Flash Targets (Deterministic)

Verified operating system partitions eligible for fast streaming and cryptographic signature verification by the LOKE bootloader.

  • BOOT / RECOVERY / VBMETA: Kernel, ramdisk, and AVB 2.0 signatures.
  • SUPER: Dynamic system, vendor, product, and odm container.
  • USERDATA: Low-level hardware erase (Opcode 0x64 Subcmd 0x07).
